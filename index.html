How it works

1. Entrepreneurs post an idea

Create a free account, describe your idea, choose whether you want sponsorship, equity investment, or both, and add your contact details.

2. Companies browse and respond

Companies search ideas by category, then send an offer with a message and their contact information.

3. You decide what happens next

Entrepreneurs see every offer on their dashboard and reach out directly to the companies they like. Agreements are made between you and the company, outside IdeaBridge.

About IdeaBridge

IdeaBridge was created to give everyone with a good idea a fair chance to be seen, no matter where they come from or who they know. We believe great ideas should not stay hidden because of a lack of money or connections. We connect people and companies, but we do not take part in or guarantee any deal.

Frequently asked questions

Is IdeaBridge free?

Yes. Creating an account, posting ideas and sending offers are free.

Who can see my idea?

Signed-in members can see the ideas and contact details you post. Please do not share secrets you are not comfortable making visible to other members.

Does IdeaBridge guarantee funding?

No. We introduce entrepreneurs and companies. Any agreement is made directly between them.
