Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information IdeaBridge ("we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using IdeaBridge you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Account information: your email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we cannot see them.

Content you post: your name, idea title and description, category, funding preference, and the contact details you choose to share. When you send an offer to an entrepreneur, we also store your company name, message and contact details.

Technical information: like most websites, our hosting and service providers may receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type and device information.

How we use your information

We use it to run the service: to create and secure your account, to show ideas to other members, to deliver offers to entrepreneurs, and to improve and protect IdeaBridge. We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see your information

Ideas you post, including the name and contact details you enter, are visible to other signed-in members. Offers you send are visible only to you and the entrepreneur who receives them. Do not post anything you want to keep private.

Services we use

IdeaBridge is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store accounts and content. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from third parties such as Google AdSense in the future. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. We will update this policy before any ads appear.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your ideas at any time from your dashboard. To delete your account and data, email us at ideabridge.support@gmail.com and we will remove it within a reasonable time.

Children

IdeaBridge is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We use reputable providers and access rules to protect your data, but no online service is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ideabridge.support@gmail.com.
